Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

arbitrage-rclone is used only by its owner to store backup archives of the owner's own project in the owner's own Google Drive.

Data accessed

The tool requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. It accesses only the files and folders it has created. It does not read, list or modify any other files, and it does not access e-mail, contacts or other Google data.

Use and sharing

Uploaded files stay in the owner's Google Drive. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train models. The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's server.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be filed as an issue in this repository.
